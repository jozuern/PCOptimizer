# Language list for websites off

## Summary
Stops websites from reading your list of Windows languages to show local content.

## How it works
This is the switch "Let websites show me locally relevant content by accessing my language list" in Settings > Privacy & security > General [1].

## Why it can help
Less data leaves this PC. No measurable effect on performance.

## Evidence
Privacy setting; it does not change frame rate or latency.

## Trade-offs & risks
Some websites may pick the language from your browser instead.

## When not to use it
Rarely needed; keep it only if websites show the wrong language.
