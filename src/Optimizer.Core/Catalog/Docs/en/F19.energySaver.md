# Energy Saver is on while plugged in

## Summary
::: status Problem
Windows Energy Saver is on while the PC is plugged in. It locks the power mode, dims the screen and turns off transparency. The effect on games is not measured.
:::
::: status Ok,Unknown,Info,Unsupported
Checks that Windows Energy Saver is off while the PC runs on mains power.
:::

## Why it matters
Energy Saver replaces Battery Saver since Windows 11 24H2 and can be set to stay on while the PC is plugged in [1]. While it is on, you cannot change the power mode, the screen brightness is reduced and transparency effects are off [1]. When a device is unplugged and low on battery, Energy Saver also blocks background apps and delays some updates and tasks [1]. Windows does not document which processor settings it uses while plugged in, so the effect on games is not measured. For gaming, the locked power mode is the part you notice: you cannot switch to "Best performance" while Energy Saver is on.

## How we detected it
We read the Windows power status (`SYSTEM_POWER_STATUS.SystemStatusFlag`), which reports whether Energy Saver is active [1], and whether the PC is on mains power. On laptops the check only runs when Windows reports mains power for sure. An organization can also force Energy Saver to stay on through a policy [2]; then you cannot change the setting yourself.

## How to fix
1. Open **Settings > System > Power** (on laptops: **Power & battery**).
2. Open **Energy saver**.
3. Turn off **Always use energy saver**. On laptops you can keep the automatic switch-on at a low battery level.

## How to check the fix
Run the scan again. Energy Saver should be shown as off.
