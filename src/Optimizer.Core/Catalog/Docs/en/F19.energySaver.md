# Energy Saver is on while plugged in

## Summary
::: status Problem
Windows Energy Saver is on while the PC is plugged in. It limits background activity and processor performance and locks the power mode.
:::
::: status Ok,Unknown,Info,Unsupported
Checks that Windows Energy Saver is off while the PC runs on mains power.
:::

## Why it matters
Since Windows 11 24H2, Energy Saver also exists on desktop PCs. When it is on, Windows switches to a power-saving processor policy, reduces background activity and locks the power mode setting. On a laptop on battery that extends runtime. Turned on permanently ("Always use energy saver"), it costs performance in games on every PC.

## How we detected it
We read the Windows power status (`SYSTEM_POWER_STATUS.SystemStatusFlag`), which reports whether Energy Saver is active, and whether the PC is on mains power.

## How to fix
1. Open **Settings > System > Power** (on laptops: **Power & battery**).
2. Open **Energy saver**.
3. Turn off **Always use energy saver**. On laptops you can keep the automatic switch-on at a low battery level.

## How to check the fix
Run the scan again. Energy Saver should be shown as off.
