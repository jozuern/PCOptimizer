# Tips, suggestions and welcome screens off

## Summary
Turns off tips, suggested content in Settings and Start, and the welcome experience after updates.

## How it works
These are the user switches behind "Show me suggested content in the Settings app" (Settings > Privacy & security > General), the tips and welcome experience switches in Settings > System > Notifications > Additional settings, and "Show recommendations for tips, shortcuts, new apps, and more" in Settings > Personalization > Start. Microsoft does not document these registry values. The matching policies, such as "Do not show Windows tips", work only on Enterprise and Education [1].

## Why it can help
Fewer interruptions. No measurable effect on performance.

## Evidence
Privacy setting; it does not change frame rate or latency.

## Trade-offs & risks
You no longer see tips for new Windows features.

## When not to use it
If the tips help you discover new features.
