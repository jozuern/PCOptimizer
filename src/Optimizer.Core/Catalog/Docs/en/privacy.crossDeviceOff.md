# Continue experiences on other devices off

## Summary
Turns off the Connected Devices Platform, which lets apps continue tasks across your devices and share nearby.

## How it works
The policy "Continue experiences on this device" is set to off [1].

## Why it can help
Less data leaves this PC. No measurable effect on performance.

## Evidence
Privacy setting; it does not change frame rate or latency.

## Trade-offs & risks
Nearby sharing and handing tasks between devices stop working.

## When not to use it
If you use nearby sharing.
