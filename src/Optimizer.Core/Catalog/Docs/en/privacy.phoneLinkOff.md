# Phone-PC linking off

## Summary
Stops this PC from linking to a phone (Phone Link) to sync messages, photos and notifications.

## How it works
The policy "Allow Phone-PC linking on this device" is set to off [1].

## Why it can help
Less data leaves this PC. No measurable effect on performance.

## Evidence
Privacy setting; it does not change frame rate or latency.

## Trade-offs & risks
Phone Link and continuing tasks from your phone no longer work.

## When not to use it
If you use Phone Link.
